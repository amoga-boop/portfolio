Selected Projects

01 · Class Project

Syringe Assembly & Structural Analysis

Designed a two-part syringe assembly (barrel + plunger) with limit-distance mates constraining plunger travel to a realistic 1–8 in range. Assigned ABS material and ran a static FEA simulation with an 80 N axial actuation force to evaluate structural integrity under injection loads.

Max resultant displacement: 3.08 × 10⁻² mm — well within safe tolerances for a medical delivery device.

02 · Design Project

Tenecteplase Nasal Spray — Emergency Drug Delivery

Modified a standard nasal spray device to serve as an emergency delivery system for Tenecteplase, a thrombolytic agent for life-threatening blood clots. The redesign addresses the drug's viscosity, dosage precision, and the need for rapid non-invasive administration when IV access may be unavailable — such as in pre-hospital stroke or pulmonary embolism response.

Clinical impact: enables faster thrombolytic delivery in stroke and PE emergencies without IV access.
